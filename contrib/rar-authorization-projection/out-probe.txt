OAuth RAR authorization_details → CLC grants
Members: RFC 9396 §2/§3 (checked 2026-10-17)

1. Mapping
   oauth-rar/rar-v1:payment:transfer    params=map[amount:5000 currency:[CNY] identifier:[acct-1234]]
   oauth-rar/rar-v1:payment:refund      params=map[amount:5000 currency:[CNY] identifier:[acct-1234]]
   residual: locations [https://api.bank.example]: a where-it-may-be-used constraint has no CLC slot

2. Operations
PASS RAR-001 transfer 3000 CNY                    want=allow got=allow
PASS RAR-002 transfer 6000 CNY (above bound)      want=deny got=deny/params_exceed_grant
PASS RAR-003 transfer 3000 USD (not the enum)     want=deny got=deny/not_in_enum
PASS RAR-004 refund 1000 CNY (second action)      want=allow got=allow

Reading: actions multiply into grants, type-specific fields become params,
and `locations` has no CLC slot.  A binding profile must state both the
type→scheme naming rule and what happens to locations (carrier-enforced).
