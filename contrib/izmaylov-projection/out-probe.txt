6. Residuals (carrier, enforcement point or not yet projected)
   - permission containment (actions, limits, requires, never, validity window) is not evaluated in the signature stage
   - countersignature and approval conditions are carrier obligations
   - cancellation and log append-only guarantees are outside this probe
   - passkey origin/rpId binding is a WebAuthn verifier obligation
   - amount and period accounting are enforcement-side and are never reported as a signature success
   - countersignature and approval conditions are not modelled in this stage
   - cancellation state is not modelled in this stage
   - passkey origin/rpId binding and signature verification belong to stage 1
   - amounts and periods are verifier arithmetic over the supplied log; the executor's reserve/commit accounting is a separate concern
   - approval signature verification against the permission's issuer is modelled, not executed
   - countersignature signature verification against the service's keys is modelled, not executed
   - approval's 'slip' and 'details' matching are not modelled
   - cancellation acknowledgement and the stricter timed-cancellation rule are not modelled
   - log append-only proofs and signed tree heads belong to stage 1
   - log append-only consistency proofs between tree heads are not modelled
   - tree-head signature verification belongs to stage 1
   - Merkle tree shape/leaf ordering is pinned by this fixture; the draft leaves the log's construction to the log operator
   - this stage recomputes roots from the leaf set; a wire-format consistency proof (e.g. RFC 6962) is not verified
   - tree-head signatures belong to stage 1
   - log operator behaviour (leaf ordering, duplicate handling) is pinned by this fixture

Reading: `failed` is a check that ran and rejected; `unavailable` is a check
that could not be run.  Izmaylov's answer 2 ("was everything verified?")
is `verified=all|partial`, answer 1 is `problem=none|found`, and answer 3
(within permission) is deliberately reported as not evaluated until the
permission-containment stage lands.
